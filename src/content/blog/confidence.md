Confidence not only applies when you are good in something. Confidence is something when you:

- are good at something,
- when you are bad at something
- when you are mediocre at something and know you can improve
- when you are mediocre at something that your possibilities to improve are low.

Confidence is the acceptance, it’s being confident with yourself, whatever that means to you.
