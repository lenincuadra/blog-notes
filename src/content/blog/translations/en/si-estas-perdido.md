---
title: 'If you’re lost'
description: 'Remember that you’ve been lost before, and you know how to get out of it.'
---

Remember that you’ve been lost before, and you know how to get out of it. The easiest thing to do again is to start your thinking from zero, zero prejudice or favoritism toward anything, just observe so objectively that you forget your personality for a few moments. Think blank and paint your thoughts again, somewhere a bias is doing you great harm.
